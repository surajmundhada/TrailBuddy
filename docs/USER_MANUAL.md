## Getting Started

### Welcome to TrailBuddy!

TrailBuddy is India's most trusted local travel guide platform, connecting you with verified local experts who can make your travel experiences authentic, safe, and memorable.

### What Makes TrailBuddy Different?

- **✅ Verified Guides**: All guides undergo background verification and ID verification
- **🤖 AI-Powered Planning**: Get personalized itineraries using advanced AI
- **🛡️ Women Safety**: Special safety features and women-friendly guides
- **💬 Real-time Chat**: Communicate directly with guides before booking
- **💳 Secure Payments**: Safe and transparent payment system
- **📖 Local Stories**: Authentic experiences shared by local guides

## User Registration

### Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit [TrailBuddy](https://trailbuddy.com)
2. Click **"Sign Up"** in the top right corner
3. Fill in your details:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Email Address** (will be verified)
   - **Phone Number** (Indian mobile number)
   - **Password** (minimum 6 characters)
   - **Role Selection**: Choose between:
     - **Traveler** - Book guides and plan trips
     - **Local Guide** - Offer your services as a guide

### Step 2: Email Verification

1. Check your email for a verification link
2. Click the link to verify your email address
3. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder
4. Request a new verification email if needed

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

1. Upload a profile photo (optional but recommended)
2. Add your travel preferences
3. Set your location for better recommendations

## Booking a Guide

### Step 1: Select a Guide

1. Browse guides and select one that matches your preferences
2. Review their profile, ratings, and availability
3. Click **"Book Now"** or **"Chat"** to discuss your requirements

### Step 2: Chat with Guide (Recommended)

1. Use the chat feature to discuss:
   - Tour itinerary and preferences
   - Special requirements
   - Customization options
   - Any questions about the tour

2. This helps ensure the guide can meet your expectations

### Step 3: Complete Booking Form

1. **Tour Details**:
   - Start date and end date
   - Start time and end time
   - Number of travelers
   - Special requirements or preferences

2. **Review Pricing**:
   - Guide's hourly/daily rate
   - Platform fee (15%)
   - Total amount

3. **Confirm Booking**:
   - Review all details
   - Accept terms and conditions
   - Proceed to payment

### Booking Status

Your booking will go through these stages:
- **Pending**: Awaiting guide confirmation
- **Confirmed**: Guide has accepted the booking
- **In Progress**: Tour is currently ongoing
- **Completed**: Tour has finished
- **Cancelled**: Booking was cancelled
- **Refunded**: Payment was refunded

## Payment Process

### Payment Methods

TrailBuddy accepts:
- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay
- **UPI**: All major UPI apps
- **Net Banking**: All major Indian banks
- **Wallets**: Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay

### Payment Security

- **SSL Encryption**: All transactions are encrypted
- **Razorpay Integration**: Secure payment gateway
- **Data Protection**: Your payment information is never stored
- **Refund Policy**: Clear refund terms and conditions

### Payment Process

1. **Initiate Payment**: After booking confirmation
2. **Enter Payment Details**: Secure payment form
3. **Verify Payment**: OTP or 3D secure verification
4. **Confirmation**: Receive payment confirmation
5. **Receipt**: Get detailed receipt via email

### Refund Policy

**Full Refund** if:
- Booking is cancelled by the guide
- Booking is cancelled 48+ hours before start time

**Partial Refund** (85%) if:
- Booking is cancelled 24-48 hours before start time

**No Refund** if:
- Booking is cancelled less than 24 hours before start time
- Tour has already started

## AI Trip Planner

### Access AI Planner

1. Navigate to **"AI Trip Planner"** in the menu
2. Answer a few questions about your trip

### Planning Input

Provide these details:
- **Destination**: City or region you want to visit
- **Duration**: Number of days for your trip
- **Budget**: Total budget in INR
- **Travelers**: Number of people traveling
- **Preferences**: 
  - Heritage & Culture
  - Adventure & Activities
  - Food & Cuisine
  - Shopping
  - Relaxation
  - Photography
- **Accommodation**: Hotel, hostel, or homestay preference

### AI Output

The AI planner provides:
- **Day-wise Itinerary**: Detailed schedule for each day
- **Hidden Gems**: Lesser-known attractions and experiences
- **Local Insights**: Cultural tips and local recommendations
- **Food Recommendations**: Must-try local dishes and restaurants
- **Budget Breakdown**: Estimated costs for activities
- **Travel Tips**: Practical advice for your destination

### Customization

1. Review the generated itinerary
2. Request modifications if needed
3. Save or share your itinerary
4. Book guides for specific activities
